# Рекомп — brand & asset brief

## The product
- **Name:** Рекомп (Latin spelling for stores and URLs: **Rekomp**). From «рекомпозиция тела»
  (body recomposition): losing fat while keeping or building muscle.
- **What it does:** an offline meal planner and food diary for body recomposition. It shows
  high-protein recipes that fit the day's calorie budget, what you can cook from what is at home
  («Дома»), a shopping list, step-by-step cooking mode with timers, and history of calories and
  protein.
- **Key idea:** *enough protein, controlled calories, real food from your own kitchen.*
- **Audience:** adults who train (gym, cycling, active jobs), cook at home, buy groceries at
  Lidl in Hungary; Russian-speaking first, English second.
- **Platform:** Android and iOS mobile app. Works fully offline, no account, data stays on the phone.

## Personality
Calm, practical, encouraging; not a strict diet app, no «fitness bro» aggression, no
shame. Fresh, food-first, modern Material 3. Think «a smart kitchen notebook», not «a gym».

## Logo ideas (pick one direction, keep it simple)
- A plate or bowl combined with a subtle upward/progress shape (recomposition).
- A leaf + protein drop/egg shape in green and orange.
- A rounded «Р»/«R» monogram built from a plate rim and a fork.
Requirements: readable at 48 px, no text inside the app icon, flat or soft-gradient, no
photographic detail, works on light and dark backgrounds, has a one-colour version.

## Assets needed (exact sizes)

| Store screenshots frame (optional) | 1080×1920 | device frame + caption style, to place real app screenshots in |
| Wordmark | SVG + PNG | «Рекомп» (Cyrillic) and «Rekomp» (Latin), horizontal lockup with the mark |

## Taglines (choose / adapt)
- RU: «Белок под контролем — из того, что есть дома.»
- RU: «Худей без потери мышц. Готовь из своего холодильника.»
- EN: «More protein, fewer guesses — cooked from your own kitchen.»

## Do / don't
- Do: food, kitchen, balance, progress; generous whitespace; rounded friendly shapes.
- Don't: scales, measuring tapes, flexing arms, before/after bodies, calorie-shaming, the
  default blue Expo «A» (current placeholder icon — to be replaced).
- Fonts in the app: Roboto (Android system, Material 3). A wordmark may use a rounded
  geometric sans with good Cyrillic support (e.g. Manrope, Nunito, Inter Rounded).

## Delivery
PNG files at the sizes above plus an SVG of the mark and wordmark. File names that drop
into the project: `icon.png`, `android-icon-foreground.png`, `android-icon-background.png`,
`android-icon-monochrome.png`, `splash-icon.png`, `favicon.png`.
